SECTION 1: MATERIAL ACCEPTANCE POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 ALL incoming materials must be inspected against the approved
    Material Specification Sheet (MSS) before release to production.

1.2 Materials failing specification are placed on QUALITY HOLD.
    Quality Hold materials may NOT be used in production without:
    (a) Written waiver from Quality Director (not Plant Manager)
    (b) Written approval from Process Engineering
    (c) Machine recalibration verification test run

1.3 Alloy grade mismatches are classified as CRITICAL deviations
    and may NOT be resolved by waiver. They require:
    - Return to supplier, OR
    - Machine recalibration and qualification
    - See Policy Bulletin PB-2023-09 (issued after INC-2023-008)

1.4 Waivers issued in violation of section 1.3 are invalid and
    do not provide protection from quality liability.

SECTION 3: DEFECT RATE RESPONSE PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 DEFECT RATE THRESHOLDS
  Green zone:    < 2.5%  — Normal operation
  Yellow zone:   2.5% – 4.0% — Investigate within 48 hours
  Orange zone:   4.0% – 6.0% — Stop affected line within 4 hours
  Red zone:      > 6.0%  — Immediate production halt

3.2 When defect rate enters Orange or Red zone:
  (a) Production on affected machine/line halted immediately
  (b) Root cause investigation initiated within 1 hour
  (c) Quality Manager notified within 30 minutes
  (d) No resumption without root cause identified and corrected

3.3 CAUTION: Policy section 3.2 mandates production halt for
  defect rates above 6.0%. AI-generated recommendations to
  continue production above this threshold MUST be reviewed by
  a human Quality Manager before implementation.

SECTION 5: SUPPLIER QUALIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 New suppliers require a 30-day qualification period including:
  - 3 consecutive batches meeting specification
  - Machine compatibility test run
  - Quality Engineering sign-off

5.2 Approved supplier list is maintained by Procurement.
    Deviations from approved supplier list require VP approval.
