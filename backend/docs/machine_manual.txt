SECTION 4: MATERIAL COMPATIBILITY AND OPERATING PARAMETERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 APPROVED MATERIAL GRADES
The ProCut-17 is calibrated for the following aluminum alloy grades:
  • Aluminum Alloy 6061 (Primary designation)
  • Aluminum Alloy 6063
  • Aluminum Alloy 5052

WARNING: Alloys outside the 6000-series, particularly the 7000-series (e.g., 7075),
have significantly higher hardness (90-120 HRB) and tensile strength (>500 MPa).
Processing 7000-series alloys on this machine WITHOUT recalibration will:
  1. Increase cutting friction and heat generation
  2. Cause excessive spindle bearing wear
  3. Raise operating temperature above safe thresholds
  4. Result in micro-warping of precision components → DEFECTS

4.2 OPERATING TEMPERATURE THRESHOLDS
Normal operating range:     65°C – 78°C
Warning threshold:          78°C – 82°C (Notify maintenance)
Critical threshold:         > 82°C  (Stop production, emergency maintenance)
Automatic shutdown:         > 90°C

The spindle bearings are rated for sustained operation at ≤ 80°C.
Sustained temperatures above 80°C will cause thermal expansion and
micro-warping of precision cutting surfaces within 48-72 hours of exposure.

4.3 VIBRATION LIMITS
Normal:    ≤ 2.0 mm/s
Warning:   2.0 – 3.0 mm/s (inspect within 24h)
Critical:  > 3.0 mm/s (stop and inspect immediately)

4.4 MATERIAL HARDNESS COMPATIBILITY
Approved hardness range:    78 – 88 HRB (Rockwell B scale)
Hard metals (> 88 HRB) will cause accelerated spindle wear and require
recalibration of feed rates and cutting speeds before use.

SECTION 7: DEFECT CAUSE MATRIX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.1 ELEVATED DEFECT RATES — DIAGNOSTIC GUIDE

Symptom: Defect rate > 3% sustained over 48 hours
Possible Causes (in order of frequency):
  1. Material specification mismatch (wrong alloy grade)
  2. Spindle bearing wear or thermal damage
  3. Cutting tool wear or breakage
  4. Coolant contamination
  5. Operator calibration error
  6. Incoming material dimensional non-conformance

7.2 CORRELATION: TEMPERATURE AND DEFECT RATE
Historical data from field deployments shows a strong positive correlation
between sustained operating temperature > 80°C and defect rate increase.
In documented cases, defect rates rose 2-4x within 5-7 days of sustained
high-temperature operation.

Corrective action: Identify heat source, replace bearings if damaged,
verify material compatibility, recalibrate feed rates.
